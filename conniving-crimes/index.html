Conniving Crimes

Crime. Mysteries. Thrillers. Gay detectives, gay suspects, and now and then a villain with very good taste. You want to know what this shelf’s about? Meet Tom Ripley. Patricia Highsmith put him in The Talented Mr Ripley, a charmer who wanted another man’s life so bad he took it. Smooth, sharp, dangerous. Most of the books here are like that. Keep your eyes on the hands.

Crime, mystery and thrillers with gay detectives, gay suspects and the occasional glamorous villain. The patron saint of this shelf is Tom Ripley, Patricia Highsmith’s charming killer in The Talented Mr Ripley, who wants another man’s life so badly he takes it. Expect the same mix of menace, desire and good tailoring.
