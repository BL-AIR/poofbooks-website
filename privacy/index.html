Privacy

What this site collects, which is very little.

We don't sell your data and we don't show ads on this site. We may advertise on Facebook and Instagram, and only with your permission does that advertising connect back to this site. You don't need an account to browse or to buy. When you order, we keep what we need to get your books to you, and nothing more.

The site also measures visits, but only if you say so, and it has a mailing list you can choose to join.

The Looker List

If you join the Poof Books Looker List, we keep your first name and email address so we can tell you when the shop opens and send the occasional early look at the stock. That's all the list is for.

The list is run on Zoho Campaigns, and your details are also kept in our Zoho CRM so we can keep track of who has asked to hear from us. Zoho stores them in its Australian data centre. Nobody joins without confirming: the first email asks you to click a link, and until you do, you're not on the list.

Every email has an unsubscribe link at the foot. Your address is never sold, never traded, and never passed to anyone except the service that delivers the emails. If you'd like your details deleted altogether, write to the address at the end of this page.

Your orders

When you buy from us, the checkout asks for your name, email address and delivery address. If your order includes a book from Exquisite Erotica, it also records that you ticked the box to say you're 18 or over. We use these to send your books, to email your receipt and tracking details, and to help if something goes wrong. Ordering doesn't put you on the Looker List; that's a separate choice.

Your order details are kept in our order database, hosted by Cloudflare. We keep order records for five years, because Australian tax law requires it, and then delete them.

To deliver an order, we pass your name and delivery address to whoever sends the parcel: Australia Post, or the book distributor that ships it straight to you (for example, Ingram in the United States). They use it only to deliver. Receipts and order emails are sent for us by ZeptoMail, Zoho's email service, from LilOtter@poofbooks.com.

Payments

Payments are handled by Stripe. Your card details go straight from the payment form to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number. We receive only what we need to match a payment to an order: the amount, the currency you paid in, the card type and its last four digits. If you pay with Apple Pay, Google Pay or Link, that also goes through Stripe.

If you choose to pay in your own currency, Stripe works out the conversion. Stripe also uses payment details to detect fraud, and may store and process them outside Australia. Stripe's handling of your data is governed by its own privacy policy.

Reader reviews

Book pages carry reader reviews and star ratings, run on Hyvor Talk. Reading them stores nothing.

You don't need an account to post. You can review as a guest under any name you like, including a made-up one, and an email address is optional. Give one only if you'd like to hear when someone replies. If you'd rather, you can sign in with a free Hyvor account instead.

When you post, Hyvor keeps the name you chose, your review and rating, your email address if you gave one, and your IP address. The IP address is never shown to anyone. We use it only to stop spam and to block someone who keeps posting abuse. So a guest review is anonymous to other readers, but not completely untraceable.

Every review is read by us before it appears, and we may decline any that are abusive, off-topic or spam. Hyvor stores reviews on its servers in Germany and doesn't sell them or use them for advertising. To have a review of yours removed, write to the address at the end of this page.

Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Tag Manager and, through it, Google Analytics, to understand how many people visit and which pages they read. Google then receives information about your visit: your IP address, the pages you look at, roughly where in the world you are, and what browser you use.

Also with your permission, we use Microsoft Clarity, which records how pages are used (clicks, scrolling, how far down a page people read) so we can see what works and what doesn't. Clarity masks anything typed into a form and doesn't collect names or contact details.

With the same permission, Google Analytics also hears how the shop is used: which shelves and books are looked at, what goes into the cart, how far a checkout gets, and what an order was worth (never your name, address or card). It also receives what you type into the quick search (a title, author or ISBN), so we can see which books people look for and can't find. What you write in Big Daddy's book finder stays in your browser: we count only how many words it had and how many books matched.

We use these to answer questions like is anyone finding the site. We don't use them to identify individuals, and we have no way of doing so.

If you decline, Clarity doesn't run at all, and Google Analytics receives only an anonymous signal that a page was viewed: no cookie, and nothing that links one visit to the next. Nothing under Statistics is used for advertising.

Google's and Microsoft's handling of the data is governed by their own privacy policies, not ours.

Marketing

With your permission under Marketing, we use the Meta Pixel. It tells Meta (Facebook and Instagram) that you visited and which pages you looked at, so we can measure our ads and show them to people who might like the shop. If you decline, the Meta Pixel doesn't run. Meta's handling of the data is governed by Meta's own privacy policy, not ours.

Facebook and Instagram

Poof Books has a Facebook Page and an Instagram account. If you message us, comment on a post, or write to us there, Meta shows us your public name and what you wrote, and we may keep a record of it in our customer system (Zoho CRM, in its Australian data centre) so we can reply and remember who we've spoken to. We don't record likes or follows. We use these records only to answer you and, if you've asked, to tell you about the shop. To have a record deleted, write to the address at the end of this page.

Address lookup at checkout

When you start typing your street address at checkout, what you type is sent to Google (Google Maps Platform) so it can suggest matching addresses. When you pick one, Google returns the suburb, state and postcode to fill in the form. Google sees only the address you type, not your name, email or order. You can ignore the suggestions and type your address in full instead. Google's handling of this data is governed by its own privacy policy.

What the host sees

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like every web server, GitHub's receives the ordinary information your browser sends in order to deliver a page: your IP address, the page you asked for, the time, and your browser type. GitHub uses this to serve the site and to defend it against attack. We have no access to those logs and no ability to identify you from them. GitHub's own privacy statement covers what they do with it.

Your rights

If you're in Australia, the UK, the European Union, or anywhere with similar laws, you have the right to ask what information we hold about you, to have it corrected, and to have it deleted (apart from order records we must keep for tax purposes). Given how little there is, this is usually a short conversation.

Children

Nothing here is directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect anything from anyone under the age required by their local law.

One shelf, Exquisite Erotica, holds explicit fiction written for adults. It asks visitors to confirm they're adults before going in, and orders that include those books ask the buyer to confirm they're 18 or over. That's a self-declaration, not an identity check.

Changes

If we start collecting anything new, such as customer accounts, this page will be updated to say so before that happens, not after. It's kept in the same public repository as the site itself, so its history is a matter of public record.
