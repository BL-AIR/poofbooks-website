About this site

Who we are, where our book information comes from, and how to put it right when it’s wrong.

Poof Books is an online bookshop for gay men, curated in Melbourne. We want more gay men reading more gay literature, especially men who don’t read much yet, and we try to help each reader find the particular book he’ll love.

We have expansion plans to include new dedicated sites catering for the other wonderful letters in our community alphabet, but we need to get this one humming along first; we also need to find partners that represent those particular readers.

Our curators are illustrated and voiced with AI tools. The books, and the choices, are human.

Where our book information comes from

Most of what you read about a book here is supplied by its publisher. We add our own layer on top: shelves, picks, reviews and corrections. Where the two disagree, ours wins.

On the siteWhere it comes from
Title, author, publisher, publication date, formats, sizes, page countThe publisher, through TitlePage, the Australian Publishers Association’s book database
The story (description), author biography, critics’ quotesThe publisher, through TitlePage
PriceThe publisher’s recommended retail price in Australia, unless we set our own
AvailabilityThe book’s Australian distributor, through TitlePage
Subjects and audienceThe publisher’s subject codes (Thema and BISAC), which we sometimes correct
CoverThe publisher, through TitlePage. Where none is supplied, Open Library
Shelf, book of the month, curators’ picks and reviewsPoof Books
Author’s pronounsThe author’s own words where we have them. Otherwise the publisher’s author biography (if it says “he” or “she”). Otherwise “Not stated”
Reading timeEstimated from the page count (about 300 words a page) until the author gives us a word count
Commissioned reviewsReviewers we pay, always marked as commissioned
Reader reviewsReaders like you

What goes on the shelves

Printed books in English that publishers describe as relating to gay men. We leave out children’s and teen books. Some books (manga, translations, and books also aimed at lesbian, bisexual or trans readers) aren’t on a shelf until we’ve looked at them, but you can still find them with search.

Getting it right

Publishers’ information isn’t always right, and we’d rather know. If something about a book looks wrong, from an author’s pronouns to a description or a price, or even whether the book belongs on the shelf or the site — email BigDaddy@poofbooks.com and we’ll fix it.

Authors: we’ll be in touch to introduce the shop and ask you to check your book’s details. You don’t need to wait for us, though.
