Oh, you’ll love it here! Magic, myth, monsters and men, in worlds where anything can happen, and usually does before breakfast. If you’ve never read The House in the Cerulean Sea, stop everything. TJ Klune’s buttoned-up caseworker is sent to inspect an island orphanage of magical children, and finds a family, and a love, he never saw coming. That’s my favourite kind of spell: the kind that makes you kinder.
