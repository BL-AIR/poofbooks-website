Help

Who to write to, and what to expect.

Orders, delivery and returns

Lil Otter in the Mailroom looks after every parcel: LilOtter@poofbooks.com. Please include your order number (it starts with PB-).

Everything else

Questions about the shop, a book, your details, or a complaint: BigDaddy@poofbooks.com.

A shelf or a curator

Each shelf has its curator's address at the foot of its page. Write to them about their shelf, a review or a book you think belongs there.

Delivery

You'll see the delivery options and their prices at checkout, worked out for your address and your books, before you pay. Within Australia we use Australia Post (Parcel Post or Express Post). For the United States, books are sent from our distributor's US warehouse. Other countries are priced at checkout too.

A multi-book order may arrive in more than one parcel, because some books come straight from our distributors. You won't pay extra for that.

If you're outside Australia, your country may charge import taxes or duties when the parcel arrives. Those are set by your government or carrier, not by us.

Paying

Prices are set in Australian dollars. At checkout you may be able to pay in your own currency, with the amount and rate shown before you pay. We never see or store your full card details.

Something wrong with your order?

A damaged, faulty or wrong book: send Lil Otter a photo and your order number, and you'll get a replacement or a refund. A parcel that hasn't arrived: let him know and he'll chase it. Changed your mind: you have 14 days to send a book back unread.

The details are in our Refunds & Returns policy, part of the Terms of Sale.

Your details

To see, correct or delete the details we hold about you, or to leave the Looker List, write to BigDaddy@poofbooks.com. See our Privacy statement.
