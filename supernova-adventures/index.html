Supernova Adventures

Strap in, crewman! Space opera, first contact, time travel, queer futures: every book on this shelf is a ticket somewhere new. Our grandmaster is Samuel R. Delany, and his Stars in My Pocket Like Grains of Sand is the big one. Six thousand worlds, and two men who discover that each is the other’s perfect match. From the old pulp classics to tomorrow’s worlds, they’re all aboard. Pick one and let’s launch!

Science fiction for and about gay men: space opera, first contact, time travel and queer futures, from the old pulp classics to tomorrow’s worlds. The shelf’s grandmaster is Samuel R. Delany, whose Stars in My Pocket Like Grains of Sand imagines a galaxy of six thousand worlds in which two men discover that each is the other’s perfect match.
