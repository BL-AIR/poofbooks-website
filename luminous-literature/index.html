The Art of Surrender

Blair Purvis

Ayman Saliba arrives in Morocco looking for permission. Lucas Pfifer arrives already fluent in concealment. In the heat and steam of a hammam, two men meet where anonymity makes desire possible — and from Gibraltar to the snowfields of Switzerland and the ruinous appetites of Berlin, they are drawn into each other in ways that expose every imbalance between them.
