Please check the details and tell us if anything is wrong. A few things our publisher feeds can't tell us, and only you can:

Pronouns. We reviewed your About the Author bio and use the pronouns you use there. If your bio uses none, please tell us how you prefer to be addressed.

Word count. This is not a publishing trick question. We use it to estimate how long a fast, medium and slow reader would take to read your book. Some readers like it long, some like it short. A quick flick through the pages on a bookshop shelf would have told them roughly the same thing. An approximate figure is fine.

Where and when. Where is your book set, and what time period or periods does it cover?

Your website. So readers can find more of your work.

Your photo. If you'd like readers to see you, upload an author photo on the first form. It is optional.

Bedfellows. Each month we pair a writer and a reader to answer the same set of standard questions. Would you like to take part?

First page. In a bookshop, a customer may read the first few paragraphs to get a feel for the prose. May we share your first page? Send as much as you're comfortable with. Because the site is publicly accessible, if your first page is on the spicy side, a more SFW section works well.

Keywords. We use your description to power our advanced search, matching the right reader to the right book. You can add up to 20 keywords to help. The underlined words are the keywords that do the work:
