History, memoir, biography, politics, ideas: the true stories of gay men’s lives, told straight, as it were. Now, if you read only one book from this shelf, make it… oh, the green one… The Velvet Rage, by Alan Downs. Splendid. It explains how growing up gay in a straight world shapes a man, and how to put the shame down. It’s the book gay men press into a friend’s hands. I’ve given away four copies. Or five. The number escapes me, but the book never does.
