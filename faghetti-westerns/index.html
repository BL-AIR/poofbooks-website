Faghetti Westerns

Out here we tell the stories the old Westerns only hinted at, partner: cowboys, drifters, ranch hands and lawmen, big skies and the kind of silences that say plenty. If you’ve read Brokeback Mountain, Annie Proulx’s tale of two Wyoming boys who couldn’t quit each other, you already know the territory. This shelf’s full of its kin: dusty classics, new frontier romances, and the odd standoff that ends with a kiss instead of a bullet.

Westerns with the subtext made text: cowboys, drifters, ranch hands and lawmen, wide skies, long silences and the things men never said out loud around the campfire. Think Brokeback Mountain, Annie Proulx’s story of two Wyoming ranch hands that broke the world’s heart. This shelf is where its cousins live: dusty classics, new frontier romances and the odd standoff settled with a kiss.
