The Loft

For authors who write books for and about men who love men. Authors only: it takes an author to bring an author up the stairs.

A word from J.T.P.Q.R. Tolstay-Smythe

Ah, you found the stairs. Mind the manuscripts. I'm J.T.P.Q.R. Tolstay-Smythe (yes, all of it), author of Scandal at Sea, and at the moment wrestling my new novel, My Man Max, into shape. The Loft is for authors, and there's only one rule: no one comes up here unless another author brings them. If someone sent you up, welcome. Put your name on the Author List below and you're one of us, and the next author up those stairs is yours to invite.

What the Author List is for

  • How to get your book listed with us, including for indie and self-published authors.
  • Invitations to feature in Bedfellows, where an author and a reader answer the same questions each month.
  • News from the shop that matters to authors: new shelves, reading events and changes to how we stock books.

Only author business goes to this list. If you'd like to hear about books as a reader, the Looker List in the Cabin is the one for that.
