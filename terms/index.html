R1. Your rights under the Australian Consumer Law

Our goods come with guarantees that cannot be excluded under the Australian Consumer Law. You are entitled to a replacement or refund for a major failure and compensation for any other reasonably foreseeable loss or damage. You are also entitled to have the goods repaired or replaced if the goods fail to be of acceptable quality and the failure does not amount to a major failure.

This policy explains how we put those rights into practice. It adds to them and never takes anything away. Customers outside Australia also keep any rights their own consumer law gives them.

R2. Faulty, damaged, misprinted or wrong books

R2.1 If a book arrives damaged, has a printing or binding fault (missing, repeated or blank pages; a loose binding), doesn't match its description, or isn't the book you ordered, email LilOtter@poofbooks.com with your order number and a photo of the problem.

R2.2 Please tell us as soon as you reasonably can. There is no deadline that removes your rights.

R2.3 You choose a replacement copy or a full refund, including the original delivery cost for that book. If the problem is minor, we may offer a replacement first.

R2.4 In most cases you won't need to send the book back. If we do need it (for example, to make a claim with a printer), we'll email you a prepaid return label or refund your return postage.

R3. Parcels that don't arrive

R3.1 If your parcel hasn't arrived by the end of its estimated delivery window, contact Lil Otter. We'll check with the carrier and our distributor.

R3.2 If the parcel is confirmed lost, or hasn't arrived 10 business days (Australia) or 20 business days (overseas) after the end of the estimate, we'll send a replacement or give a full refund, whichever you prefer.

R3.3 If tracking says "delivered" but you don't have it, check with neighbours and your building's mail area first. We'll then lodge an enquiry with the carrier and work it out with you.

R4. Change of mind

R4.1 You aren't entitled to a refund just because you've changed your mind, but we offer one anyway.

R4.2 Tell us within 14 days of delivery, and return the book unread and in the condition it arrived (no writing, creases, broken spine or damage).

R4.3 We'll email you the return address. You pay the return postage. We recommend a tracked service, because the book is your responsibility until it reaches us.

R4.4 When it arrives in good condition, we'll refund the book price within 5 business days. The original delivery cost isn't refunded for change of mind.

R4.5 Pre-orders and backorders can be cancelled for a full refund at any time before dispatch.

R5. How refunds are paid

R5.1 Refunds go back to the card or wallet you paid with, in the currency you paid in. Your bank may take a few days to show it. If your card was charged in a different currency from your own, exchange rates may have moved since you paid, and we can't cover the difference.

R6. Who to contact

Lil Otter handles every return: LilOtter@poofbooks.com. If you're not happy with how we've handled it, write to BigDaddy@poofbooks.com. You can also contact Consumer Affairs Victoria or your local consumer agency at any time. For other help, see Help.
